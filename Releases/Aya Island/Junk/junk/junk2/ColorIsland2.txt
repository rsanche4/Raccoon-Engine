The game's vision:
"Exploring a mysterious island, with a mysterious secret"

The idea is that you are a tourist that has been sent here to collect information about its residents. You have a Camera which takes pictures of the place. And you also have a note that basically keeps a log of all the different things regarding each of the people in the island, their culture, all sorts of things. Their behaviors all that. 

As such, you are to explore every bit and cranny of the island and it will lead to the story of course being interconnected. With people with a keen eye. It should be atmospheric, and each part of the island should have the feeling or music etc. Or ambiance.
